Study of the validity and limitations of turbulence Kolmogorov theory applied to
real turbulent flows, using time series data of the down-stream velocity from a
wind tunnel. The study highlights, through numerical simulations and data analysis,
how the theory breaks due to intermittency phenomena. The presentation
obtained a grade of 5.75/6.
