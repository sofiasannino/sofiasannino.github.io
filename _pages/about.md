I am Sofia, I am 24 and I have been living in Switzerland for one year, finishing my master. You can read what I did on LinkedIn, this space is to explain why.
When I decided to study Mathematical Engineering I read a book called "Atlas of AI" (Yale Press, 2021) that starts more or less with this line: *Artificial intelligence is not artificial nor intelligent*. It was talking about the societal and environmental exploitation generated by this technology. But will the disruption be inevatibly bad for general people? Is there a way AI will use its type of intelligence to develop a more just society?  

Time has passed since then and the potential of AI is now huge, just like the gap to fully understand it, interpret it and deploy it to improve life conditions for everyone. 
I would like to contribute to fill this gap, hoping that one day we will be able to know exactly what happens during training, just like the numerical algorithms I used to focus on during the Bachelor, and fully interpret models, as well as to put humans first when implementing them.
No more black boxes.
